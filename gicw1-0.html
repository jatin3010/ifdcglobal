GICW 1.0, held in 2019, left an indelible mark on the fashion industry, showcasing a stunning array of talent and captivating ramp walks by some of the most prominent faces in the business. Renowned personalities like Divya Khosla Kumar, esteemed filmmaker and style icon, and Kashish Thakur Pundir, winner of the popular reality show "Roadies," graced the runway with their striking presence. Adding to the allure, Asmeet Patel, a rising star, mesmerized the audience with his charisma and impeccable style. GICW 1.0 brought together these eminent personalities, making it an unforgettable showcase of fashion brilliance. Witness the magic of GICW, where glamour meets artistry, and redefine your perception of runway glamour.
